# Senior Talent Acquisition Partner – Executive Search

**Client:** Nexus Strategic Advisors Pvt. Ltd.  
**Location:** Mumbai / Remote  
**Experience:** 6–12 years in executive search or high-volume tech hiring  
**Budget CTC:** ₹18–28 LPA  

## Mandate
Own end-to-end mandates from sourcing through offer. Heavy use of Naukri, LinkedIn Recruiter, and CEIPAL. Partner with practice leads on CXO and senior specialist searches.

## Must-have
- Hands-on Boolean / X-ray sourcing on Naukri, Shine, LinkedIn
- Stakeholder management with client interview panels
- Experience writing candidate dossiers and running structured screens (notice, CTC, relocation)
- Comfort with ATS hygiene (CEIPAL or similar)

## Nice to have
- AI tools for screening (Claude, ChatGPT)
- Understanding of fee-based search economics (retained / contingency)
- Exposure to recruiter productivity tools (TeamLogger, timesheets)

## Success profile
High submission-to-interview conversion, low no-show rates, and evidence of closed senior mandates.
