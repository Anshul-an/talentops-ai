# Changelog

All notable changes to TalentOps AI are documented here.

## [v0.1.0] - 2026-10-27
### Added
- Resume parser with multi-engine support (LLM + heuristic fallback).
- Executive fitment scorer with domain relevance, recency, and stability metrics.
- Candidate screening desk chat agent.
- Mock interview scheduling and automated reminder queue.
- Executive MIS Excel export (3 sheets: Pipeline, KPI Leaderboard, Channel Matrix).
- SQLite storage layer with dedup matching on email, phone, and LinkedIn.
- Full-text search and recruiter notes endpoints.
- Detailed health and metrics endpoints.
